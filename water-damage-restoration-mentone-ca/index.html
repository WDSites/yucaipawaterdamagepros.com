Mentone roots: orchards, Mill Creek Road and the river bottom

The Mentone Corporation formed in 1886 and laid out a town beside the Santa Ana River, borrowing the name of Menton, France. Its early economy was navel oranges and olive trees, and that history still shapes the town: long streets, larger lots and homes of very different ages sit side by side. A house built in the early twentieth century and a modern one down the road can need completely different drying plans.

Mill Creek Road becomes the town's eastern main street, and Mentone Boulevard runs west toward Redlands. If you live along either corridor, the first step in any water call is still the same: stop the source and phone the emergency line. We can discuss the rest when we arrive.

Why county jurisdiction matters for your repair

Because Mentone has no city council, the county handles its government functions, including building rules. If your repair goes beyond drying and cleanup, ask San Bernardino County which permits apply before you hire a rebuild contractor. We handle the water side, and you handle the paperwork with the county.

Your water provider is worth checking, too. Mentone addresses can have different water providers, so check your water bill for your provider's name and keep the number handy for a main shutoff.

When water is spreading in Mentone

Spreading water can come from a pipe, a roof, or an appliance, and in older Mentone houses it can travel inside walls for a long time. Call the emergency line the moment you see a stain growing or a floor starting to lift. Pumps and extraction units get the bulk of the water out, and drying equipment handles what is left in the structure.

If a storm caused the water, the same urgency applies. A neighbor's drainage can push runoff toward your yard, and a clogged gutter can send water behind siding. Emergency water removal is the right starting page for anything wet and standing.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Stop the water at the source

Close the fixture shutoff, then the main valve. In older Mentone homes the shutoff may be outside near the meter, so check there if you cannot find one inside.

Power off where it is dry and safe

You can switch off wet-room breakers only from a dry spot at the panel. Water dripping from a ceiling fixture is the signal to back away and ring 911 or your power utility.

Lift valuables and soft items

Move bedding, books and electronics onto dry furniture. Roll up area rugs if they are not heavy, and leave the carpet for the crew.

Keep people and pets out

If the water is backing up from a drain or came in with mud, keep everyone out of it. Call the line, and we will tell you what to expect.
