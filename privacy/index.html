Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Yucaipa Water Damage Pros collects when you visit this site or call our line.

What this site collects

This website does not have forms, accounts or comment fields. We do not ask you to type personal information into the site. Like most sites, the web host may log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view.

Calls to our line

When you call (909) 505-3870, we ask for details needed to help you, such as your address, the type of water problem and a way to reach you. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information

We use call information to understand your situation, dispatch a crew and follow up on the job. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may display an embedded Google Map. Those services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask what we have on file or ask us to delete call records by phoning us or emailing contact@yucaipawaterdamagepros.com. Where law requires, we will honor those requests.

Children

This site is meant for adults dealing with property damage and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@yucaipawaterdamagepros.com, or by mail to 34420-34488 Yucaipa Blvd, Yucaipa, CA 92399.
