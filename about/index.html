About Yucaipa Water Damage Pros

We run a 24/7 emergency line for water damage, flood cleanup and mold calls in Yucaipa and the towns around it. This page explains how a call works and what we will and will not do.

Who we are

A local line for a stressful day

Yucaipa Water Damage Pros is a dispatch and restoration service based at 34420-34488 Yucaipa Blvd in Yucaipa, California. We take calls about water on floors, wet ceilings, burst pipes, flooding and mold, and we send licensed and insured restoration professionals to handle the work.

We keep this site call-only on purpose. There are no forms to fill out and no waiting for an email reply. You phone the line at (909) 505-3870 and talk to a person who asks what you see, where the water is coming from and whether anyone is in danger.

We serve Yucaipa first, then the towns around it: Redlands, Calimesa, Mentone, Beaumont and Loma Linda. Each town has its own page with local notes, because a leak in a historic Redlands house is a different job from a foothill slab in Calimesa.

What we do

Services we cover

Most calls fall into a few groups. Standing water gets emergency water removal. Wet walls and floors get water mitigation, which is the drying and stabilizing phase. Soaked belongings and contaminated areas get water damage cleanup, and storm or groundwater problems fall under flood damage restoration.

Once the structure is dry, water damage repair covers drywall, flooring, paint and trim. Where moisture has led to mold, mold remediation follows the published ANSI/IICRC S520 standard for the work, and we encourage you to ask any company whether its crews follow S520 for mold and S500 for water.

Honest limits

What we do not do

Water damage restoration is not waterproofing. If water keeps coming in because of grading, a failing foundation or a poorly sealed wall, restoration cleans up after it, and a waterproofing or drainage contractor fixes the cause. We will tell you plainly when that is the situation.

We do not promise an arrival time, and we do not promise what your insurer will approve. Coverage depends on your policy. The usual pattern is that a sudden pipe failure is treated differently from a slow leak or a flood, so read your policy or call your insurer before you assume anything.

We do not offer equipment rentals, and we do not make health claims about mold. Questions about your health belong with a physician.

Working with insurance

Documentation that helps your claim

Photograph and film the affected rooms, the source of the water and each damaged item before cleanup starts, with the date showing. Save receipts, list what was lost, and keep ruined items on hand until your insurer clears you to dispose of them. Crews also record moisture readings and what was removed, and those notes can support your conversation with the insurer.
