Older Redlands homes and what water does to them

Redlands was founded in 1881 and grew up around citrus, which is why so many blocks still hold Victorian-era houses and early bungalows. Those homes often have plaster walls, lath, old cast or galvanized supply lines and layers of paint that have been patched for decades. A slow leak behind that kind of wall can soften plaster long before a stain shows, so a small brown ring on a ceiling deserves a call, not a wait-and-see week.

Older construction changes how we dry a house. Plaster holds moisture differently from modern drywall, tongue-and-groove floors can cup when only one face dries, and original trim is worth saving when it can be saved. We take moisture readings first and decide what to remove after, so you do not lose historic material that could have been dried in place.

If your house is a newer build on the east side of town, the pattern flips. Supply lines, water heaters, refrigerator ice-maker lines and washing machine hoses fail more often than anything in the walls. Either way, water mitigation in the first 24 to 72 hours is what keeps a leak from becoming a rebuild.

When water is spreading in Redlands

Standing water, a ceiling that is dripping, or a floor that feels spongy underfoot all point to active water, and the first move is the same everywhere. Find the source, shut off the water, and call the emergency line before the damage travels along framing and into the next room. Emergency water removal with pumps and extraction units is what pulls the bulk of the water out so drying can start.

In a plaster house, speed matters even more. Plaster and lath can wick moisture upward, so a leak that started at a second-floor bathroom may be showing on the ceiling of the room below an hour later. If you see water coming through a ceiling light fixture, stay away from it and read the safety step in the cards below.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Stop the source

Close the fixture shutoff or the main water valve. Older Redlands homes sometimes have a stiff main valve, so turn it slowly and tell us if it will not close.

Power off only if it is safe

Flip breakers for wet rooms only when the panel sits on a dry floor and you are not touching water. A wet outlet or a dripping ceiling light means step back and phone 911 or the power utility before anything else.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, boxes and small furniture onto dry surfaces. Pull antiques and family pieces off wet floors, and leave heavy items for the crew.

Keep people and pets out

Keep everyone out of standing water from toilets, sinks backing up, or any source you cannot name. Then call the 24/7 line and tell us what you see.
