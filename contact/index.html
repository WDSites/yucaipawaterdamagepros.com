Before you call

What to have ready

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water is and how far it has spread.
  • What you think caused it, such as a pipe, a roof, an appliance or a storm.
  • Whether the water is clean, from a toilet or drain, or came in with mud.
  • Whether any outlets, light fixtures or the electrical panel are wet.
  • Whether you rent, own or manage the building.

If you cannot answer every question, call anyway. The dispatcher will ask them in order.

Safety first

Do these before or while you call

If water is near electricity or gas

Stay out. For an electrical hazard, call 911 or your power utility before you phone us. If you smell gas, get everyone outside and ring 911 or the gas utility from a safe distance. Then call us.

If it is safe, shut off the water at the fixture or the main, lift valuables off the floor and keep people and pets out of any water you cannot identify. Our emergency water removal page has the full list.
